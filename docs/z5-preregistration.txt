Rule order (the first that holds is the angle; W = winner, L = loser, a break = the end of a quarter):
  1. overtime            the linescore has more than 4 periods
  2. comeback            W trailed by 10 (COMEBACK_POINTS) or more at a break before the last
  3. late_lead_change    W didn't lead at the break before the last (trailing or tied after Q3)
  4. seesaw              the lead changed hands at 2 or more breaks
  5. blowout             W won by 17 (BLOWOUT_POINTS) or more
  6. close_finish        decided by 3 (CLOSE_POINTS) or less, or a tie
  7. outgained_but_lost  L had 75 (OUTGAINED_YARDS) or more total yards than W
  8. turnovers           one team had 3 (TURNOVER_GAP) or more giveaways than the other
  9. front_runner        W led at every break
 10. routine_win         none of the above
